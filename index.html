HOW TO PLAY

🧟
STOP THE ZOMBIESZombie volleyball players shuffle at the net in 3 lanes. If one reaches the net you lose a ❤️.
🏐
NON-STOP SETSYour setter keeps feeding you balls. You jump by yourself. Swipe up when the ring turns gold at the top of the jump.
↖⬆↗
PICK A LANESwipe up-left = left lane, straight up (or tap) = middle, up-right = right lane.
💥
TIMINGPERFECT = huge hit + knockback. Too early = net. Too late = out. Hit in a row to grow your combo.
🎁
POWER-UPSZombies sometimes drop 🔥 fireballs, ✨ triple spikes, ❄️ freeze and ❤️ hearts.
