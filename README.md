# GCVC United Zombie Spike

Play: https://northernelectronic.github.io/gcvc-zombie-spike/ · Android APK: see Releases.

You are the GCVC United hitter at the net. The setter keeps feeding you balls; cartoon zombie volleyball players
shuffle toward the net in three lanes. When the ring turns gold at the top of your jump, swipe up-left, up or
up-right to spike into that lane. Perfect timing hits harder and knocks zombies back. Don't let them reach the net!
